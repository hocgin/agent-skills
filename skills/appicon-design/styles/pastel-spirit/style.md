# 柔彩灵物 `pastel-spirit`

> 参考：`reference.png`。参考图只用于提炼设计语言，它的主体（幽灵 / 小鸟状角色与左后方两片尾片）属于参考 IP，见「参考 IP 排除」。

## 设计 DNA（可复用）

| 维度 | 规范 |
|---|---|
| 配色 | 背景奶白 `#FFFFF7`；主体渐变柔珊瑚 `#D98C7C` → 蜜桃粉 `#F0A18D` → 浅杏 `#FFD39E`；后置软片淡紫 `#CBBBF4` 与奶黄 `#FFE59A`；眼睛暖白 `#FFF9F5`；边缘乳白 `#FFF1D6` |
| 造型语言 | 单个圆鼓、流线、略不对称的萌物剪影；只有两颗略倾斜白色椭圆眼，无嘴无瞳；后置最多两片圆角软片 |
| 材质 | 轻微半透明的薄树脂 / 磨砂亚克力凝胶：正面柔润、边缘透亮、厚度浅，轮廓清晰 |
| 光影 | 左上大面积柔光，乳白细透光边；下缘淡杏反射；接触影浅而弥散，似轻浮；无强点状高光 |
| 构图原则 | 一个主体 + 后置软片 + 奶白留空；主脸是唯一焦点；位置见「版式变体」 |
| 质感 | 连续细腻的低对比渐变，通透不失色，无颗粒绒毛 |
| 细节密度 | 两颗白眼、≤2 个轮廓识别凸起、≤2 片后置软片；无瞳孔、嘴、粗描边与漂浮装饰；无文字 |
| 情绪 | 治愈、轻盈、梦幻、温柔 |

**签名手法**：① 蜜桃渐变的浅厚度半透明萌物；② 只有两颗倾斜白色椭圆眼；③ 后置淡紫 / 奶黄软片与乳白细亮边。

## 参考 IP 排除

- 原主体：幽灵 / 小鸟状角色——圆身体、一侧小尖尾、顶部 1–2 个软凸起。
- 原布局：主体略偏右居中约 70% 宽，两片软片从左上与左中露出。

## 主体转译规则

把核心功能变成一个**由业务隐喻决定轮廓**的小灵物（**不要画成幽灵或小鸟**）：水滴（喝水）、云朵（睡眠 / 同步）、叶芽（习惯 / 成长）、音符（声音）、月牙（夜晚）、花苞（情绪）、贝壳（收藏）、蒲公英绒球（放松）……

- 只保留在小尺寸可辨的轮廓特征，不添加真实器官。
- 后置两片软片按隐喻转译为叶片、光环、波纹、花瓣或一对简化功能标签，不能成为独立角色。
- 避免套用 `cozy-face` 的满版脸与抱物构图。

## 版式变体

```composition:float-center
Creamy near-white background fills the canvas edge to edge. One complete rounded mascot sits at the exact center, occupying about 62–68% of the canvas width, with ample empty space around its intact silhouette. Two small pastel pieces peek out from behind its right side, one above the other, staying secondary to the face. Mostly frontal view with shallow visible thickness and a softly floating presence. Both eyes clearly visible in the upper half of the main form. No surrounding scene or scattered decorations.
```

```composition:rise
Creamy near-white background fills the canvas edge to edge. One rounded mascot rises gently toward the upper right, tilted about 15 degrees, occupying about 65% of the canvas width and placed slightly below and left of center. Two pastel pieces trail behind its lower-left edge like a soft wake. The whole silhouette stays intact with breathing room on all sides. Both eyes face the direction of travel. No surrounding scene or scattered decorations.
```

```composition:nestle
Creamy near-white background fills the canvas edge to edge. One large soft pastel lavender backing shape sits behind the lower half of the composition like a cushion, and a smaller buttery yellow piece peeks from behind its top right. The rounded peach mascot rests on the cushion at center, occupying about 58% of the canvas width, fully visible. Both eyes clearly visible in the upper half of the mascot. No surrounding scene or scattered decorations.
```

## 可变元素

- 萌物的业务剪影与轮廓识别凸起
- 两颗眼睛的倾斜角度和位置
- 后置软片的形状、角度与露出面积（颜色固定，位置随版式）

## 风格约束（style）

```style
Gentle pastel mascot rendered as a softly rounded, shallow, subtly translucent tinted-resin form with a frosted acrylic-gel softness. Smooth tonal color transitions from muted coral (#D98C7C) through peach pink (#F0A18D) to pale apricot (#FFD39E) toward the bottom. A thin milky cream (#FFF1D6) luminous rim defines the silhouette without a dark outline. Only two slightly tilted warm-white (#FFF9F5) oval eyes, no pupils and no mouth.
```
