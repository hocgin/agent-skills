# 叠贴搭档 `buddy-tiles`

> 参考：`reference.png`。参考图只用于提炼设计语言，它的两个卡片角色及其「左下大 + 右下小 + 右上星芒」布局属于参考 IP，见「参考 IP 排除」。

## 设计 DNA（可复用）

| 维度 | 规范 |
|---|---|
| 配色 | 背景浅青 `#AEF0FC`；角色 A 亮青蓝 `#00BFEF`（内面冰蓝 `#DDF8FD`、眉线深青 `#007D9C`）；角色 B 酸橙绿 `#93D900`（内面淡黄绿 `#F1FFDD`）；厚边与眼白近白 `#FAFEFF`；瞳孔近黑 `#161A1C`；唯一跳色星芒黄 `#FFD000`。A / B 谁大谁小可按项目互换 |
| 造型语言 | 扁厚、大圆角的拟人贴片；外圈宽厚白边，彩色内框包住浅色面部；大眼白、大瞳孔、粗眉线，表情有反差 |
| 材质 | 不透明光滑的软塑料 / 搪胶厚贴片，面部轻微内凹，白边圆润凸起 |
| 光影 | 左上柔光，白边柔亮高光，内框轻微遮蔽；重叠处浅软接触影 |
| 构图原则 | 一主一辅组成**一个**紧凑视觉组，有遮挡关系；眼睛在安全区；具体位置见「版式变体」 |
| 质感 | 干净平滑的 2.5D 卡通渲染，无颗粒 / 金属 / 透明玻璃 |
| 细节密度 | 1 枚白厚边黄色四角星芒 + ≤6 个白色圆头短线 / 点；每个角色内部 ≤1 个无文字简化符号 |
| 情绪 | 机灵、活泼、亲近、互动 |

**签名手法**：① 拟人厚贴片 + 宽白色软边 + 浅色内凹面部；② 一主一辅、有遮挡的搭档关系；③ 有反差的眼神 + 唯一黄色星芒。

## 参考 IP 排除

- 原角色：蓝色大卡片 / 屏幕脸角色 + 绿色小卡片角色（半眯眼的大角色配好奇睁眼的小角色）。
- 原布局：大角色从左下斜向探入约 70% 宽、小角色在右下前景约 45% 宽、星芒在右上。

## 主体转译规则

把项目核心能力转成**一对新形状**的拟人贴片，形状必须由业务隐喻决定，**不要默认画成卡片 / 屏幕**：对话气泡 + 回复小气泡、书本 + 书签、文件夹 + 便签、云 + 雨滴、手柄 + 小按钮、日历页 + 勾选章、拼贴方块 + 圆点……

- 主次可以互换颜色（绿大蓝小）；表情可重新设计（例如主角好奇、搭档淘气），不要照搬「大的半眯眼、小的睁眼」。
- 业务无双人关系时，用「能力 + 结果」组成一对。

## 版式变体

```composition:side-by-side
Pale cyan background fills the canvas edge to edge. One cohesive paired emblem centered on the canvas: the dominant character stands upright on the left, about 60% of the canvas width; the smaller companion, about 40% wide, stands on the right, overlapping the dominant one's right edge in front. Both are fully visible within the central 80% safe area, their eyes turned toward each other. The single sparkle sits at the upper left with a few short dashes. Mostly frontal view with shallow thickness.
```

```composition:piggyback
Pale cyan background fills the canvas edge to edge. One cohesive paired emblem: the dominant character sits in the lower center, about 65% of the canvas width, slightly cropped by the bottom edge; the smaller companion, about 35% wide, perches on its top edge, tilted playfully. Both faces stay inside the central safe area, the companion looking down and the dominant one looking up. The single sparkle sits at the upper right with a few short dashes. Mostly frontal view with shallow thickness.
```

```composition:peek-behind
Pale cyan background fills the canvas edge to edge. One cohesive paired emblem: the dominant character is centered, upright and fully visible, about 62% of the canvas width; the smaller companion, about 35% wide, peeks out from behind its upper-left shoulder, partly hidden. Both sets of eyes remain within the central 80% safe area. The single sparkle sits at the lower right with a few short dashes. Mostly frontal view with shallow thickness.
```

## 可变元素

- 两个角色的业务形状、简化符号、主次与颜色互换
- 表情（视线、眉角、小弯嘴）与轻微旋转
- 版式与星芒 / 短线的位置（随版式）

## 风格约束（style）

```style
Playful 2.5D cartoon illustration of two opaque rounded soft-plastic sticker characters with thick raised near-white (#FAFEFF) rims and gently recessed pale face panels. One character is bright cyan (#00BFEF) with an icy blue (#DDF8FD) face and deep teal (#007D9C) expressive brows; the other is lime green (#93D900) with a pale yellow-green (#F1FFDD) face. Large near-white eyes, near-black (#161A1C) pupils, broad rounded brows and a tiny curved mouth create two contrasting expressions. Smooth softly beveled edges, soft upper-left lighting, gentle rim highlights and subtle contact shadows at overlaps. Pale cyan (#AEF0FC) background, one yellow (#FFD000) four-point sparkle with a thick white rim and at most six white capsule dashes or dots. Clean polished opaque surfaces, no grain. Lively, cheeky and companionable mood.
```

## 负面约束（negative）

```negative
isolated non-overlapping characters, more than two characters, equally sized characters, full body limbs, realistic device interface, readable screen content, app window, thin outline, flat monochrome symbol, transparent glass, chrome, metallic finish, realistic skin, furry surface, grain, sharp corners on the characters, dark background, rainbow fill, excessive sparkles, long hard cast shadows
```

## 参考 IP 排除（avoid）

```avoid
large cyan card-shaped character entering diagonally from the lower left, small lime card-shaped character at the lower right in front, sparkle at the upper right, half-closed sleepy eyes on the large character, phone-screen faces
```

## 验收清单

- [ ] 一主一辅组成一个标志，遮挡关系符合所选版式
- [ ] 宽白色凸边、浅色内面与软塑料厚度清晰可见
- [ ] 角色形状来自业务隐喻，不是两张卡片 / 屏幕
- [ ] 浅青背景，只有一枚黄星芒与少量白短线 / 点，无文字
- [ ] 与参考图并排对比：角色形状、表情分配和布局均不同
