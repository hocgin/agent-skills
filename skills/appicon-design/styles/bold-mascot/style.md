# 粗线萌徽 `bold-mascot`

> 参考：`reference.png`。参考图只用于提炼设计语言，它的主体（带笑脸的拼图块）属于参考 IP，见「参考 IP 排除」。

## 设计 DNA（可复用）

| 维度 | 规范 |
|---|---|
| 配色 | 背景暖白 `#FFFBF3`；轮廓与五官深梅棕 `#422234`；主体渐变：紫 `#8771F7` → 粉 `#F39BC0` → 珊瑚粉 `#FFA69F` → 金黄 `#FFDA68`，方向随版式（纵向或对角） |
| 造型语言 | 单个完整、圆润、略不规则的徽章剪影；描边宽约画布 4–5%，圆头圆接角；最多 3 处凸凹特征，负形开口宽大 |
| 材质 | 插画式不透明软胶贴章，正面近乎平面，边缘极浅厚度 |
| 光影 | 左上柔光，轮廓细微亮边，下方偏右短而柔的暖灰投影 |
| 构图原则 | 一个完整主体 + 暖白留空；五官嵌在主体内；位置见「版式变体」 |
| 质感 | 平滑连续渐变、利落轮廓，无纹理颗粒 |
| 细节密度 | 两颗深色短胶囊眼 + 一条宽弧笑线（表情可调）；无腮红、手脚、散落装饰；无文字 |
| 情绪 | 友好、乐观、俏皮、轻松 |

**签名手法**：① 深梅色极粗圆润描边与同色极简五官；② 紫 → 粉 → 金的连续渐变填色；③ 暖白留空与浅软胶贴章投影。

## 参考 IP 排除

- 原主体：拼图块（含卡口凸起与凹槽）+ 上半部笑脸。
- 原布局：拼图居中约 70% 宽、轻微倾斜、纵向渐变、短眼 + 大弯笑线居中上。

## 主体转译规则

把业务隐喻压缩成一个能加五官的**新剪影**（**不得使用拼图块**；模块 / 插件类改用插头、齿轮、六边形蜂巢块等，同样避免乐高积木这类知名造型）：书签（收藏）、气泡（沟通）、云朵（同步）、文件夹（整理）、灯泡（创意）、调色盘（创作）、口袋（收纳）、火苗（动力）……

- 保留最多 2–3 处识别结构，所有尖角软化。
- 表情可在「两短眼 + 宽笑线」基础上调整：眨一只眼、O 形小嘴、侧视，但始终用同色粗线。

## 版式变体

```composition:centered
Warm off-white background fills the canvas edge to edge. One complete centered emblem occupies about 66–72% of the canvas width with generous breathing room on all sides. Straight-on view, upright with no tilt, the gradient running vertically from violet at the top to golden yellow at the bottom. The simple face sits in the middle of the silhouette. No surrounding objects or decorative marks.
```

```composition:big-crop
Warm off-white background fills the canvas edge to edge. One emblem is large, about 90% of the canvas width, its lower part extending past the bottom edge while its whole upper silhouette and identifying feature remain visible. The gradient runs diagonally from violet at the upper left to golden yellow at the lower right. The simple face sits in the upper half, glancing slightly sideways. No surrounding objects or decorative marks.
```

```composition:lean
Warm off-white background fills the canvas edge to edge. One complete emblem occupies about 65% of the canvas width, leaning about 12 degrees to the right as if mid-bounce, placed slightly left of center to balance the lean. The gradient runs vertically from violet at the top to golden yellow at the bottom. The simple face sits in the upper half, turned toward the lean direction. No surrounding objects or decorative marks.
```

## 可变元素

- 单个徽章的业务剪影、凸凹结构与负形
- 版式（居中 / 大尺寸裁切 / 倾斜）与渐变方向
- 表情的细节变化（仍为同色粗线）

## 风格约束（style）

```style
Friendly illustrated mascot badge with an opaque almost-flat soft-rubber face and extremely thick deep plum-brown (#422234) contours, about 4–5% of the canvas width, with fully rounded caps and joins. A smooth continuous fill transitions from violet (#8771F7) through pink (#F39BC0) and coral pink (#FFA69F) to golden yellow (#FFDA68). Use the same deep plum-brown for a minimal face drawn in the same bold rounded line, such as two short capsule eyes and one broad curved smile. The silhouette is plump, simple and gently irregular with a few large rounded protrusions or cutouts. Very shallow raised edges, faint upper-left edge light and a short soft warm-gray contact shadow below and to the right. Warm off-white (#FFFBF3) background, crisp clean contours, seamless gradients, no grain. Friendly, optimistic and playful mood.
```
